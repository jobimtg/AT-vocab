### Post 3 — Airport English: Check-In

```text
Airport English doesn’t need to be complicated.

Practice this short check-in dialogue:

Staff: May I see your passport?
You: Sure. Here it is.

Staff: Do you have any checked luggage?
You: Yes, I have one suitcase.

Staff: Window or aisle seat?
You: A window seat, please.

Try reading it out loud 3 times:

1. Slowly
2. Naturally
3. With confidence

Free Travel English preview lessons are in the link in bio.
```

### Post 4 — Pronunciation Tip: TH

```text
Pronunciation tip: TH sound

Many English learners replace “th” with “s,” “t,” or “d.”

Try this:

1. Put your tongue lightly between your teeth.
2. Push air out gently.
3. Practice slowly first.

Words:

✦ think
✦ thank you
✦ three things

Sentence:

“Thank you for helping me.”

Practice 5 times today.
```

### Post 5 — Phone Plan English

```text
Need a phone plan in Canada?

Here is a useful dialogue:

You: I’m looking for a phone plan.
Staff: How much data do you need?
You: I need about 20GB per month.
Staff: Do you want a monthly plan?
You: Yes, please. No contract if possible.

Useful phrase:

“No contract if possible.”

This is a simple sentence, but it can help you sound clear and prepared.

More Canada Life English practice is available through the link in bio.
```
