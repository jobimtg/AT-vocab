Situation 1 — At the Check-In Counter 場景一：辦理登機手續
Smooth 順暢版
Situation 情境
You are at the airport check-in counter. Show your documents, check in your suitcase, and ask for your seat and gate information. You are flying to Frankfurt, Germany.
Person A
Check-in Agent 航空公司職員
  • Ask for passport & ticket
  • Ask about luggage count
  • Weigh suitcase on scale
  • Offer window or aisle seat
  • Give boarding pass + gate
Person B
Traveler 旅客（你）
  • Show passport and ticket
  • Tell how many suitcases
  • Ask if weight is okay
  • Request a window seat
  • Ask where the gate is
Full Model Dialogue — Read aloud, then practice without the script
A
Good morning! May I see your passport and ticket, please?
B
Good morning! Yes, here you are.
A
Thank you. How many suitcases are you checking in today?
B
I have one suitcase to check in.
A
Please place it on the scale. Your bag is within the weight limit. Would you like a window seat or an aisle seat?
B
A window seat, please. I love the view!
A
Of course! Here is your boarding pass. Your gate is B12. Boarding time is 10:45.
B
Thank you! Which terminal is Gate B12 in?
A
It is in Terminal 2. Turn left and walk straight ahead.
B
Thank you very much! Have a nice day!
A
You are welcome! Have a pleasant flight!
boarding pass 登機證
check-in 辦理登機
suitcase 行李箱
gate 登機門
window seat 靠窗座位
terminal 航廈
weight limit 重量限制
boarding time 登機時間
Now You Try! 換你說
Same situation — but you have two suitcases and you want an aisle seat. Change those two details and say the full dialogue again!
Situation 2 — Flight is Delayed! 場景二：班機延誤！
Problem 問題版
Situation 情境
You are at the airport and see on the departures board that your flight is delayed. You go to the information desk to ask for help. Stay calm and polite!
Person A
Information Staff 資訊服務人員
  • Ask for flight number
  • Explain the delay reason
  • Give new departure time
  • Direct to correct gate
  • Offer to help further
Person B
Traveler 旅客（你）
  • Explain your situation
  • Give your flight number
  • Ask why it is delayed
  • Ask new departure time
  • Ask where to wait
Full Model Dialogue — Read aloud, then practice without the script
B
Excuse me! My flight is delayed. Can you help me?
A
Of course! What is your flight number?
B
My flight number is LH 728. It goes to Frankfurt.
A
Yes, Flight LH 728 is delayed by 2 hours. There is bad weather at the departure airport.
B
I see. What is the new departure time?
A
The new departure time is 1:30 PM. Please go to gate C7.
B
Gate C7? Which terminal is that in?
A
It is in Terminal 1. Take the shuttle bus from this terminal.
B
Okay. Where do I wait? Is there food there?
A
Yes, there is a café near Gate C7. Your boarding time will be 1:00 PM.
B
Thank you so much. I was very worried!
A
No problem. Have a safe trip!
delayed 延誤
flight number 班機號碼
departure 出發
gate 登機門
terminal 航廈
boarding time 登機時間
Now You Try! 換你說
Same problem — but now your flight is cancelled (not just delayed). How do you ask the staff what to do next? Try to keep going!
🗣️ Speaking Practice — 口說練習（選 3 題回答）
Level 1 · Yes/No
1. Have you ever been to an airport? What do you remember?
Answer yes or no, then add 1–2 sentences to explain.
Level 1 · Simple
2. Do you prefer a window seat or an aisle seat? Why?
Pick one and give 1 reason. Use: "I prefer ___ because ___."
Level 2 · Short Answer
3. You arrive at the airport and your flight is delayed. What do you do?
Try to say 3–4 sentences. Use: "First, I will... Then, I will... Finally..."
Level 2 · Short Answer
4. Describe what happens at the check-in counter. What do you say and do?
Try to describe 4–5 steps in order. Use vocabulary from this lesson.
Level 3 · Open
5. Imagine you are at the airport alone going to Germany. Describe your feelings and what you do from arrival at the airport until boarding the plane.
Challenge: speak for 1 minute without stopping. It is okay to make mistakes!
