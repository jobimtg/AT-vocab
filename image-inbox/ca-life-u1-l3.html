Warm-up

What phone plan do you need?

In Canada, you may need to talk about a SIM card, monthly data, coverage, contract length, and extra fees. This lesson helps you ask simple but important questions before you choose a phone plan.

Lesson goal: By the end of this lesson, you can ask about phone plans, compare options, and explain a simple problem with your bill or mobile service.

Quick questions

Do you need more data or cheaper monthly payments?
Do you prefer prepaid or a monthly plan?
What do you usually use your phone for?
What would you ask before signing a phone contract?

Pronunciation Spotlight

Phone-plan pronunciation

Focus on clear stress and difficult consonant groups in common phone-plan words.

un-LIM-it-ed
Stress the second syllable: unlimited data.
ACT-i-va-tion fee
Say the /v/ clearly in activation.
CON-tract vs con-TRACT
Noun: a contract. Verb: to contract.
COV-er-age

Canadian Culture

Phone plans in Canada

Phone plans in Canada can feel expensive compared with many countries. Always ask what is included, how long the contract is, whether there are activation fees, and what happens if you use extra data.

Tip: Before you agree to a plan, ask for the total monthly cost before tax and ask whether the price changes after a promotion ends.
