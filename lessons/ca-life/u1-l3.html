Warm-up

What phone plan do you need?

In Canada, you may need to talk about a SIM card, monthly data, coverage, contract length, promotion price, credit check, and extra fees. This lesson helps students ask before signing, compare options, and solve simple problems after getting a plan.

Lesson goal: By the end of this lesson, students can choose a phone plan, ask about the total monthly cost, compare prepaid and monthly plans, and explain simple billing or service problems.

Quick speaking questions

Do you need more data or cheaper monthly payments?
Would you choose prepaid or a monthly plan? Why?
What do you usually use mobile data for?
What questions should you ask before signing a phone contract?
