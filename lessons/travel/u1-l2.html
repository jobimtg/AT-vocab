📖 Extended Reference — Security & Immigration（遇到時認識即可）
Situation 1 — At the Security Check 場景一：安全檢查
Smooth 順暢版
Situation 情境
You are going through airport security before your flight to Frankfurt. A security officer asks you to prepare your belongings for the scanner. Follow the officer's instructions calmly.
Person A
Security Officer
安全官員
  • Ask traveler to use tray
  • Tell what items to remove
  • Ask about laptop
  • Direct through scanner
  • Confirm clearance
Person B
Traveler 旅客（你）
旅客
  • Put bag in tray
  • Remove jacket, belt, shoes
  • Confirm laptop separately
  • Walk through scanner
  • Collect items after
Full Model Dialogue — Read aloud, then practice without the script
A
Good morning. Please put your bag in the tray.
B
Okay, like this?
A
Yes. Please remove your jacket and belt.
B
Sure. And my shoes too?
A
Yes, shoes too. Do you have a laptop in your bag?
B
Yes, I have a laptop.
A
Please put it in a separate tray.
B
Okay, done. Is this right?
A
Good. Now please walk through the scanner.
B
Okay. Can I get my bag now?
A
Yes, you are all clear. Collect your items on the other side.
B
Thank you very much!
tray 置物籃
remove 拿掉
jacket 外套
belt 皮帶
shoes 鞋子
laptop 筆電
separate 分開的
scanner 掃描機
Now You Try! 換你說
Same security check — but this time you do NOT have a laptop. You only have a jacket to remove. Change those parts and say the dialogue again!
Situation 2 — At Passport Control 場景二：護照查驗
Problem 問題版
Situation 情境
You have landed in Frankfurt, Germany. You are at passport control. The immigration officer asks about your visit. You feel nervous but stay polite and answer carefully.
Person A
Immigration Officer
入境官員
  • Ask for passport
  • Ask purpose of visit
  • Ask how long the stay is
  • Ask where you will stay
  • Ask about declaration
Person B
Traveler 旅客（你）
旅客
  • Show passport politely
  • State purpose of visit
  • Give length of stay
  • Name hotel or address
  • Declare or say nothing
Full Model Dialogue — Read aloud, then practice without the script
A
Good afternoon. May I see your passport, please?
B
Yes, here you are.
A
Thank you. What is the purpose of your visit?
B
Tourism. I am here for tourism.
A
How long is your stay?
B
My stay is ten days.
A
Where will you stay?
B
I will stay at a hotel in Berlin. The name is Hotel Mitte.
A
Do you have anything to declare?
B
No. I have nothing to declare.
A
Very good. Welcome to Germany. Enjoy your trip!
B
Thank you so much!
purpose 目的
tourism 觀光旅遊
how long 多長時間
stay 停留
declare 申報
officer 官員
Now You Try! 換你說
Same passport control — but this time your stay is 7 days and you will stay at a hostel in Munich. Change those two details and speak again!
🗣️ Speaking Practice — 口說練習（選 3 題回答）
Level 1 · Yes/No
1. Have you ever gone through airport security before? What do you remember?
Hint: Answer yes or no, then add 1–2 sentences about what you remember.
Level 1 · Simple Choice
2. When an immigration officer asks "What is the purpose of your visit?" — what do you say?
Hint: Use one of these: "Tourism." / "I am here for tourism." / "I am visiting Germany as a tourist."
Level 2 · Short Answer
3. Describe the steps to go through airport security. What must you remove? What goes in a separate tray?
Hint: Try to say 3–4 steps in order. Use: "First, I..." / "Then, I..." / "Finally, I..."
Level 2 · Short Answer
4. An immigration officer asks you three questions: purpose of visit, length of stay, and where you will stay. Practice answering all three in a row.
Hint: Aim for 3 complete sentences. Use vocabulary from this lesson. Try not to look at the dialogue above!
Level 3 · Open
5. Imagine you are at Frankfurt Airport. You go through security and then passport control alone for the first time. Describe the whole experience — what happens, what the officers say, and how you feel.
Hint: Challenge — speak for 1 minute without stopping. Use security AND immigration vocabulary. It is okay to make mistakes!
